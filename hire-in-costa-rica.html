A country worth hiring from

A country that traded its army for schools and rainforests, Costa Rica lives by 'pura vida' — a calm, positive, service-first ethos. Between cloud forests, volcanoes, and two coastlines, its people are famously friendly and reliable. Decades of hosting US shared-service centers have built a workforce fluent in North American business culture.
