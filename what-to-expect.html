What you can expect

Four steps. Full transparency.

Here's exactly what happens when you apply — what we do at each stage, how long it takes, and what you need to bring.

1
Step one
Application Review
We review your profile and experience against our active role openings. We're looking for alignment on background, experience level, and the type of role you're targeting — not a perfect CV.
CV and LinkedIn reviewed
Experience timeline checked
Role alignment assessed
Salary expectations noted
💡
Tip: Make sure your CV clearly shows the roles you held, the companies you worked at, and the responsibilities — not just job titles. US clients need context, not just credentials.
2
Step two
Screening & Validation
We verify your background — employment history, timelines, and role responsibilities — to make sure what's on your CV matches reality. This isn't designed to catch you out. It's designed to make you credible when a client sees your name.
Employment history verified
Timeline accuracy confirmed
Role responsibilities cross-checked
Education confirmed if required
💡
Tip: Be accurate and specific. Gaps are fine — just be honest about them. We can work with real history. We can't work with inflated history that falls apart when a client asks questions.
3
Step three
Communication & Evaluation
We evaluate your English proficiency through real conversation — not a written test. We also administer a role-specific assessment tailored to the position you're applying for. This is where we build the full picture that goes to clients.
Spoken English evaluated live
Written communication reviewed
Role-specific skills assessment
Recruiter interview conducted
💡
Tip: The English evaluation is a conversation, not a test. Speak naturally. US SaaS teams need people who can communicate fluently day-to-day — that's what we're assessing.
4
Step four
Opportunity Matching
If you pass the evaluation, you're added to our active bench — and matched with clients who are hiring for your role. We present you directly to the hiring manager with your full scorecard, removing the uncertainty of a cold application.
Added to Nearwork active bench
Presented to matching clients
Full scorecard shared on your behalf
Salary negotiation supported by Nearwork
🎉
This is the goal: You're presented to a vetted US company, with your experience fully verified and your credentials backed by Nearwork. You arrive as a pre-vetted candidate — not a cold CV in an inbox.
