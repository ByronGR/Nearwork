The search, in order

Who does what, at each stage.

We agree the timeline at intake, against the role and the market. Here is the division of work.

Stage oneIntake

We do

Take the brief: the work, the seniority, the timezone, the budget and what a strong hire looks like to you. We come back with what the market pays for it.

You do

Tell us the role and approve the target profile and salary band before we search.

Stage twoMarket mapping

We do

You do

Nothing. You get the map when the shortlist arrives.

Contact candidates, screen them against your brief and check working English. Only what passes goes in the shortlist.

You do

Interview, assess, decide and make the offer. The process is yours from here.

Questions

Before you start a search.

How many profiles do we get?

It varies by role. A common support or operations role has a deep market and produces a longer shortlist; a senior or niche technical role produces fewer, stronger profiles. We tell you what the market can support at intake, before you commit.

What exactly is the $1,800 for?

The search: role intake, market mapping with a salary benchmark, outreach, screening against your brief, an English check, and delivery of the profiles into your portal. It is a flat fee per role — not a percentage of salary and not a subscription.

Who runs the interviews?

You do. Sourcing hands you the shortlist and stops there. If you would rather we assessed and scored candidates and coordinated interviews, that is Direct Recruiting.

Is there a replacement guarantee?

Not with Sourcing. You run the assessment and make the call, so the hire is yours. Direct Recruiting carries a replacement guarantee because we run the evaluation.

Which countries do you search?

All of Latin America. Colombia, Mexico, Argentina, Peru, Chile, Brazil, Uruguay and Ecuador are where we work most, and we source across Central America and the Caribbean too. Where we look depends on the role, the timezone you need and what the market pays.

How is English checked?

A recruiter speaks with every candidate and assesses working English against the level the role needs, and the level is recorded on the profile you receive.

Can you also employ the person for us?

Yes, separately. Contractor of Record makes Nearwork the legal employer abroad and handles payroll, taxes, benefits and compliance while the person works for you.

What if we need several roles?

The fee is per role, so it scales predictably. Tell us the set of roles at intake and we sequence the searches.
