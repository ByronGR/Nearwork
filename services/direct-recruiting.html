FAQ

Questions, answered.

How fast can you fill a role?

Most roles reach a shortlist of fully assessed finalists in about 21 days. The exact timing depends on seniority and how specific the skill set is — we'll give you a realistic range on the intro call.

What if the hire doesn't work out?

Every placement includes a 3-month replacement guarantee. If it isn't the right fit, we find a replacement at no additional placement fee.

Where are the candidates based?

Colombia, working in your time zone with full overlap on your business hours. Every candidate is English-verified — typically C1 — before they ever reach you.

Do I need a subscription or long-term contract?

No. You can pay a one-time placement fee per hire with no membership and nothing to cancel. If you hire regularly, an optional membership lowers the per-hire fee — but it's always your choice.

How do you assess candidates?

Each finalist goes through a structured interview, a role-based skills assessment, a DISC profile, and a culture-fit check — all built from what you tell us in the intro call. You see every result in their profile.

Can you handle payroll and benefits too?

Yes. Start with recruiting and add EOR, payroll, benefits, and ongoing team support whenever you're ready — one partner, no new vendors to onboard.
