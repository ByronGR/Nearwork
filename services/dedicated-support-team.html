Support standards

Dedicated support is not just more agents. It is a system.

Nearwork designs the coverage model, trains the team, reviews quality, and keeps the operation aligned with the standards your customers expect.

01

Omnichannel coverage

Chat, email, phone, WhatsApp, helpdesk, social inbox, and back-office queues can be staffed under one operating model.

02

Quality assurance

Ticket reviews, tone checks, escalation audits, knowledge base gaps, and coaching loops keep quality improving.

03

Industry playbooks

We build workflows around your industry expectations, from SaaS support to ecommerce, billing, helpdesk, and customer ops.

04

Outcome reporting

Track SLAs, CSAT, volume, backlog, recurring issues, first response, resolution time, and improvement actions.
