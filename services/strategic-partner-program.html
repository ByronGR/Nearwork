How the partnership works

Your brand in front. Our engine behind.

FAQ

Partner questions, answered.

Will my client ever know Nearwork is involved?

No. The program is fully white-labeled — presentations, reports, portals, and communication all carry your brand. Nearwork never contacts your client directly.

How do rates and margin work?

You receive Nearwork's published wholesale rates and set your own client price. Whatever you charge above wholesale is your margin — we only ever bill you the wholesale rate.

What can I resell?

The full stack: direct recruiting, COR and payroll, and fully dedicated teams. Offer one service or all three under your brand.

Who handles delivery?

Nearwork does — sourcing, vetting, hiring, payroll and compliance, team leadership, QA, and reporting — all behind your brand.

Is there a minimum commitment?

No long lock-in. Start with a single placement or referral and scale into wholesale and strategic tiers as your volume grows.

How fast can I start?

Once your application is approved, onboarding and your white-label kit typically take days — then you can start selling right away.
