👤 Candidates · FAQ

Questions? We've got you.

Everything you need to know about applying, our process, and what to expect when you work with Nearwork.

🔍

Applying
01 How do I apply for a job?
+
Browse open roles at nearwork.co/jobs, click the one that fits your background, and apply directly through the platform. You'll create a free profile as part of the process.
1
Browse open roles and find one that matches your experience
2
Click "Apply now" and create your free candidate profile
3
Submit your application — our team reviews it within 2–3 business days
Browse open roles →
02 Can I apply for multiple roles at the same time?
+
Yes. You can apply to any role that matches your experience and interests. We'll review each application and move forward with the ones that are the strongest fit for each specific role.
No limit on applications
03 Is there a cost to apply?
+
No — applying and working with Nearwork is completely free for candidates. Nearwork is paid by the companies that hire through us, never by the candidates we represent.
Always free for candidates
04 Can I update my information after applying?
+
Yes. You can update your profile at any time through your Nearwork candidate account. We recommend keeping your information current — especially your experience, contact details, and any new roles you've taken on.
Log in to your account →
The process
05 What happens after I apply?
+
Our team reviews your profile and, if there's a strong match, moves you through our screening process. You'll hear from us via email or WhatsApp within 2–3 business days. Not every application will advance — but we'll always let you know either way when possible.
1
Application review — we assess your profile and experience
2
Screening — background and role alignment verified
3
Evaluation — English, skills, and role-fit assessment
4
Opportunity match — you're presented to the right client
See the full process →
06 How long does the process take?
+
It depends on the role and the client's timeline. Typically, from application to first presentation to a client takes 2–4 weeks — sometimes faster if there's an active search that matches your profile closely. We aim to move candidates forward as quickly as possible without rushing the fit.
07 Will I be notified if I'm not selected?
+
We aim to communicate clearly at every stage. If you're not advancing, we'll let you know when possible — and if we can, we'll share feedback on what would help your application for future roles. You can also track your status at any time through your candidate account.
08 How does Nearwork evaluate candidates?
+
We use a 7-stage structured vetting process — profile review, background verification, English evaluation through real conversation, a role-specific assessment, and a recruiter interview. By the time you're presented to a client, your profile is fully verified and scored.
See our 7-stage pipeline →
09 What if I don't get selected for this role?
+
Not every role will be the right match — and that's okay. Your profile stays active in our system. When a new role opens that matches your background, we'll reach out. You don't need to start the process over from scratch.
Your profile stays active
English
10 Do I need to speak English?
+
Yes. Most roles require a C1 Advanced level of English — you need to be able to communicate clearly in spoken and written English with US-based teams every day. For some support roles, B2 may be sufficient. We evaluate your English through real conversation during the process, not through certificates.
See English requirements →
11 Do I need an English certificate (TOEFL, IELTS)?
+
No certificate required. We evaluate your English directly through a live conversation with our recruiting team. How you communicate in a real interaction matters far more to us — and to clients — than a test score from months ago.
No certificate needed
12 What if my English isn't at C1 yet?
+
We recommend applying when you're confident your English is ready. If it's not quite there, create your profile anyway — it's free and keeps you in our system for when you're ready. Most people move from B2 to C1 in 3–6 months of consistent real-world practice.
Tips for improving your English →
Your account
13 Do I need to create an account to apply?
+
Yes — your Nearwork account is where we manage your application, track your status, and connect you with current and future opportunities. Creating one is free and takes a few minutes. It also means your profile stays active for future roles even after one search closes.
Create your free account →
14 How will Nearwork contact me?
+
We communicate via email and WhatsApp — based on your preference. Make sure your contact information in your account is correct and up to date so we can reach you quickly when there's a match.
15 How do I track my application status?
+
Log into your Nearwork candidate account and you'll see your current application stage, any open actions required from you, and notifications from our team. You don't need to email us to find out where you are — it's all visible in your dashboard.
Log in to check →
General
16 Do you guarantee I'll get a job?
+
We don't guarantee placement. We focus on connecting you with real, high-quality opportunities that match your profile — and being honest with you about fit. What we can promise: if you're a strong candidate, we'll work hard to find the right company for you.
17 What makes Nearwork different from other recruiters?
+
Most recruiting firms send your CV to whoever is hiring and hope something lands. Nearwork uses a structured vetting process to match you specifically to roles where your background, English level, and goals align. You arrive at a client as a pre-verified candidate — not a cold name in an inbox.
18 How much will I earn? Are salaries in USD?
+
Yes — all Nearwork roles pay in US dollars. The exact salary depends on the role, your experience level, and the client. Each job listing includes a salary range. We support the salary discussion between you and the client to ensure it reflects the market and your qualifications.
19 Are the roles fully remote?
+
Yes — all Nearwork placements are fully remote. You work from anywhere in Colombia (or wherever you're based). The companies you work with are in the US, so you'll align with US business hours for most of your working day.
100% remote
20 What timezone do I need to work in?
+
Colombia is in the CST timezone (UTC–5), which aligns well with US business hours — particularly US Central and Eastern time. Most roles require you to be available during standard US business hours (approximately 9am–6pm EST). This is one of the key reasons Colombian talent is attractive to US companies.
21 What type of contract would I have?
+
Contract structure depends on the client and role. Most placements are direct contracts between you and the client company — Nearwork facilitates the match and ensures everything is set up correctly. Details about contract type and terms are discussed when you reach the offer stage.
