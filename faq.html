FAQ

Questions before you hire with Nearwork?

Here are the common questions companies ask when hiring remote talent in Colombia with Nearwork.

Ask us directlySee pricing
Quick answer

Nearwork can recruit, assess, hire, pay, support, and report on remote talent depending on the service model you choose.

What does Nearwork do?

Nearwork helps companies hire and support remote talent in Colombia through direct recruiting, EOR/payroll, managed teams, strategic partner programs, and dedicated support teams.

Do you only recruit in Colombia?

Colombia is our core market because of talent quality, cost advantage, and US time-zone alignment. We can discuss broader nearshore needs during intake.

Can Nearwork be the legal employer?

Yes, through EOR services Nearwork can support contracts, payroll, benefits, taxes, and compliance for companies that do not have a local entity.

How fast can we see candidates?

Most roles begin seeing qualified candidates after intake and sourcing. The target is to move from brief to finalists in about 21 days for common roles.

How do assessments work?

Assessments are generated based on the opening skills and role context, then paired with DISC insights and recruiter review.

Can we manage a full team through Nearwork?

Yes. Managed Team includes dedicated talent, account management, reporting, team leads or industry leads, and ongoing support.
