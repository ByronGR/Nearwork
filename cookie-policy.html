Section 1

Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how Nearwork ("we", "our", "us") uses cookies and similar technologies when you access or use our platform and services ("Services").

By using Nearwork, you consent to the use of cookies as described in this policy. You can manage your cookie preferences at any time through your browser settings — see Section 5.

Section 2

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, or phone) when you visit a website or use a web application. They are widely used to make websites work correctly, remember your preferences, and provide information about how users interact with a platform.

Cookies do not contain executable code and cannot access other files on your device. They are read only by the website or service that created them.

Section 3

How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to:

  • Ensure the platform functions correctly and reliably
  • Keep you securely logged into your account
  • Improve user experience and platform performance
  • Understand how users interact with our Services
  • Enhance security and help prevent fraud
  • Remember your preferences across sessions
Section 4

Types of Cookies We Use

🔒 a. Essential Cookies Required
These cookies are required for the platform to operate. Without them, core features — including login, authentication, and account access — will not function. They cannot be disabled without breaking the platform.
Authentication & login Session management Account security Core functionality
📊 b. Performance & Analytics Cookies Optional
These cookies help us understand how users interact with our platform — which pages are most visited, where users encounter issues, and how features are used. This data helps us improve the platform over time.
Traffic measurement Usage patterns Performance monitoring Error tracking
⚙️ c. Functional Cookies Optional
These cookies remember your preferences and settings so you don't have to re-enter them every time you visit. Disabling them may mean the platform doesn't remember your preferences between sessions.
Language preferences Communication preferences UI settings
🔗 d. Third-Party Cookies Optional
We use trusted third-party services that may set their own cookies to support platform functionality. These providers collect limited technical information as necessary to deliver their services. We do not control the cookies set by third parties.
Firebase Vercel
Section 5

Managing Cookies

You can control, restrict, or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View which cookies are stored on your device
  • Delete existing cookies
  • Block cookies from specific or all websites
  • Receive notifications before a cookie is stored
Please note: Disabling essential cookies will affect platform functionality. Features such as logging in, maintaining your session, and accessing your account may not work correctly if essential cookies are disabled.

For instructions on managing cookies in your specific browser, refer to your browser's help documentation.

Section 6

Data Collected via Cookies

Cookies on the Nearwork platform may collect the following types of information:

  • IP address
  • Device and browser type
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited and features used
  • Time and duration of visits
  • Referring URLs

This data is used solely to operate and improve our Services and is handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy. We do not use this data for advertising purposes.
