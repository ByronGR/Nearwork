Why post-hire support is critical

Hiring doesn't end when a candidate starts.

That's when expectations meet reality. Performance is validated. Adjustments are needed. Staying involved helps prevent issues before they become problems.

🔍
Catch issues early
Most hiring problems surface in the first 60–90 days. Check-ins at Month 1 catch them before they compound into something harder to fix.
📈
Validate performance against goals
The success criteria defined at kick-off become the benchmarks we review at each check-in. You see if the hire is delivering what was promised.
🔁
Make the next hire better
What we learn from your first hire shapes the second. Companies that work with Nearwork repeatedly see faster, more accurate placements over time.
🛡️
Guaranteed resolution if needed
If an issue can't be resolved and the hire doesn't work out, the 6-month guarantee activates immediately. No extra cost, no delays, no complications.
We hold our work to a higher standard

Our goal isn't placement. It's performance.

We don't just place candidates — we take responsibility for the outcome. If a hire isn't meeting expectations, we step in.

01
We identify the gap
Through monthly check-ins, we surface any performance or alignment concerns before you have to raise them yourself. We're proactive, not reactive.
02
We work with you on next steps
Depending on the issue — performance, communication, role mismatch — we help you identify the right path forward. That might be coaching support, adjusted expectations, or escalation.
03
We support the transition if needed
If the situation can't be resolved, we activate the replacement guarantee — same 7-stage process, $0 additional cost, under 21 days to a new shortlist. We make it right.
