Graduates with real-world experience

They don't just learn — they apply.

Access to education in Colombia allows many students to begin working while studying. It's common for professionals to graduate with significant real-world experience already on their CV.

🏢
Internships during studies
Most Colombian university programs require or strongly encourage internship placements — often at multinational companies operating in Colombia. Graduates enter the workforce with real employer experience.
💻
Freelance work history
A large proportion of Colombian professionals begin freelancing while studying — for US and European clients. This creates practical communication, project management, and delivery skills from an early age.
🚀
Early professional roles
Many graduates take on part-time or junior professional roles while completing their degree — arriving at their first full-time job with 1–2 years of relevant experience already behind them.
