Simplified hiring structure

Clean, compliant, straightforward.

Nearwork supports hiring international talent through contractor-based structures — reducing administrative complexity while maintaining full compliance.

01
Work with international contractors
Colombian professionals work as independent contractors — a standard structure for international remote work that your legal team already knows.
02
Standard documentation
Use W-8BEN for tax classification. No W-2s, no 1099 employment complexity. Clean paper trail for your accounting team.
03
No US employment overhead
No payroll taxes, no benefits contributions, no workers' comp — the contractor model eliminates these costs entirely.
04

We vet, evaluate, and present candidates. You review, hire, and pay. We manage the relationship from our side so you don't have to.
