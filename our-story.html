🏢 About Nearwork

Our
Story.

How Nearwork started — and why we're building a better way to hire.

Chapter 01  ·  The beginning

Where it started.

Nearwork was founded in 2024 with a simple but important goal: help companies hire better.

We began by working with local and international companies looking to build teams in Colombia. These were companies that had identified the opportunity — high-quality professionals, strong English, excellent cost efficiency, full US timezone alignment — but didn't have a reliable way to access it.

At first, it seemed like a talent problem. There weren't enough candidates, or the right ones were hard to find. But it didn't take long to realize something more fundamental was broken.

The real issue wasn't talent — it was the hiring process itself.
Chapter 02  ·  The problem

What we saw.

The more companies we worked with, the clearer the pattern became. Hiring in most organizations — regardless of size or industry — looked like this:

  • Disorganized — no defined process, no clear owner, no structured timeline
  • Reactive — responding to whoever showed up, rather than finding the right fit
  • Opaque — neither the company nor the candidate knew what was happening or why

Companies were receiving resumes without context or verification. Candidates were going through unclear evaluation processes with no feedback and no visibility. Both sides were losing time — and trust.

💡
The pain wasn't lack of talent. Colombia has a deep, growing professional class — motivated, educated, and increasingly equipped to work in international environments. The pain was that no one had built a process around that talent that actually worked for US companies.

That gap is what Nearwork was built to close.

Chapter 03  ·  The decision

What we decided to build.

We could have followed the traditional model — send CVs, collect fees, repeat. It's the path of least resistance in the staffing industry, and it works in the short term.

We chose not to.

Instead, we spent the time building something fundamentally different: a structured hiring system — not just a staffing service. One that:

  • Creates clarity for companies — with a defined process, real visibility, and honest communication
  • Respects candidates' time — with structured evaluation, clear feedback, and meaningful opportunities
  • Brings transparency to every step — through a live dashboard, recruiter notes, and post-hire follow-up

This meant building a 7-stage vetting pipeline, a client-facing Kanban dashboard, a candidate portal, a 6-month replacement guarantee, and a post-hire check-in structure. All of it, from scratch — because nothing that already existed was built to do what we needed it to do.

"We're not here for transactions. We're here for results."
Chapter 04  ·  Growth

Growing the vision.

In 2026, we began expanding our reach — making our approach more accessible to more companies and deepening our presence across Colombia's four strongest nearshore cities: Medellín, Bogotá, Barranquilla, and Bucaramanga.

Each city has a distinct talent profile. Medellín brings innovation and energy. Bogotá offers scale and corporate depth. Barranquilla provides a cost-efficiency advantage with a motivated, early-mover candidate pool. Bucaramanga delivers precision and some of the highest retention rates we've seen anywhere in the country.

Together, they give us coverage of nearly every hiring strategy a US SaaS company might need. As we grow, the goal stays the same: improve how companies hire — and how candidates experience that process.

Chapter 05  ·  Today

Where we are today.

Nearwork continues to evolve as a platform built around three principles that have never changed since we started.

We work with companies that want more than just candidates. They want a process they can trust — one where they know what's happening, why decisions are being made, and what comes next. That's what we've built, and it's what we stand behind with a 6-month replacement guarantee on every placement.

📊
What the data shows: 94% average 12-month retention across Nearwork placements. Under 21 days to first shortlist. 2–3 pre-vetted candidates per search. These aren't goals — they're the current baseline.
Chapter 06  ·  Looking forward

What's next.

We're continuing to build, improve, and expand — not just to grow Nearwork as a company, but to set a higher standard for how hiring should work in the nearshore space.

That means better tooling, deeper city coverage, more refined vetting, and a candidate experience that continues to improve as we learn from every placement we make.

Most of all, it means staying focused on the thing that started all of this: the belief that hiring can be done better — and that building the right process is what makes that possible.
