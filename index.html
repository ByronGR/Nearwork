Why Nearwork

Low-risk by design

No entity to open, no salary percentage, no surprise invoice. Here's what protects you on every hire.

Flat placement fee

You pay a fixed fee per hire — never a percentage of salary. From $1,300 to $3,490, depending on your plan.

Replacement guarantee

If a hire doesn't work out under plan terms, we source a replacement free — 3 to 6 months of cover on every placement.

Compliant employment, handled

Through our Colombia EOR we run contracts, payroll, taxes and benefits in-country — from $350 per contractor each month.

Evidence before you decide

Every shortlist includes candidate profiles, an English assessment and a results review — so you compare without chasing notes.

C1 English, verified

Every candidate meets a verified C1 English standard, so communication is never the thing that slows your team down.

One portal, full visibility

Track every candidate from profile review to hired in the Nearwork portal — one login for your whole team.

FAQ

Questions, answered

The things teams ask before their first hire. Still unsure about something? We'll walk you through it on a quick call.

Talk to our team
Where are your professionals based?

In Colombia — a deep, English-fluent talent pool in a time zone that overlaps North American business hours, with a strong culture of remote, US-facing work.

How do you vet candidates?

Every candidate goes through a multi-stage process: role-specific skills assessments, an English fluency evaluation, DISC behavioral profiling, plus reference and background checks. You see all of it in their profile before you commit.

Will they work in my time zone?

Yes. Our talent works your business hours, so there's real-time overlap with your team for standups, calls and collaboration — not just async handoffs.

What if a hire doesn't work out?

You're covered by a 6-month free replacement guarantee. If a placement isn't the right fit, we source and onboard a replacement at no additional placement fee.

Who handles payroll, taxes and compliance?

We do. Through our Colombia EOR, Nearwork acts as the employer of record — running payroll and staying compliant with Colombian labor law — so you never open an entity or chase paperwork.

How is pricing structured?

A flat monthly subscription plus a one-time placement fee per successful hire. Never a percentage of salary, and no surprise invoices.

How fast can I actually hire?

Most teams see their first vetted shortlist within about 5 business days, and complete a hire within two to three weeks — far faster than building a pipeline yourself.

Is my data and IP protected?

Yes. Every engagement is covered by NDAs, with device security and access controls in place so your systems, data and intellectual property stay protected.
