The remote hiring process

An on demand hiring system.
Not just a service.

We don't send resumes and disappear. We build a structured pipeline around your role and manage every step — so you make a confident hire, fast, with radically lower labor costs.

01
Step one
Alignment

A focused kick-off to understand what you actually need — not just the job description, but your team culture, communication style, and what success looks like at 30, 60, and 90 days. We don't guess. We understand.

Kick-off call · Role brief · Success criteria
02
Step two
Structured sourcing

Every candidate passes our 7-stage vetting: background check, C1 English evaluation through real conversation, technical or role-specific assessment, DISC profile, and AI match scoring. You receive 2–3 pre-scored profiles. Not 20 CVs to sort through.

7-stage pipeline · C1 English · DISC profile
03
Step three
Managed pipeline

Your private dashboard shows every candidate, every stage, every recruiter note — in real time. Skill radars, DISC profiles, and side-by-side comparisons. You never have to ask "where are we?" The answer is always in front of you.

Live kanban · Full transparency · No email chains
04
Step four
Offer & hire

Once you choose, we move fast. We manage alignment on both sides, equipment provisioning, payroll setup, and run post-placement check-ins at 30, 60, and 90 days. Every placement is backed by our 6-month replacement guarantee.

6-month guarantee · 30/60/90 day check-ins
