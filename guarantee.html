Why we offer this

Most staffing companies focus on making placements. We focus on making the right one.

The 6-month guarantee isn't a safety net we hope to never use. It's a reflection of the confidence we have in our 7-stage vetting process — and our commitment to your long-term success, not just the placement fee.

🎯
Our process is built to not need the guarantee
The 7-stage pipeline, English evaluation, role-specific assessment, and reference checks are all designed to prevent bad fits before they happen. The guarantee exists for the rare case they do.
🤝
We're accountable beyond the placement
Most agencies get paid when the candidate starts. Our incentive structure rewards long-term success — which means we're as invested in your hire working out as you are.
🛡️
Six months is a real commitment
Industry standard is 30–90 days with exceptions. We offer 6 months because that's when you can actually tell if a hire was right for your team, your culture, and your goals.
