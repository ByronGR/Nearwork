The business capital of Colombia

Where corporate Colombia operates.

Bogotá is the economic and corporate center of Colombia — headquarters for major corporations, financial institutions, and multinational companies. That density of professional environments produces a workforce unlike anywhere else in the country.

🏛️
Headquarters of 70% of Fortune 500 LatAm operations
Global companies chose Bogotá first when entering Latin America — creating a professional culture shaped by international standards from day one.
🏦
Colombia's financial and banking center
Home to the Bolsa de Colombia, all major banks, and the country's largest financial services sector — producing finance, ops, and compliance professionals at scale.
🌐
International trade and commerce hub
Bogotá handles 40%+ of Colombia's international trade — giving its workforce deep familiarity with cross-border business, compliance, and communication.
🚀
Growing tech and startup district (Chapinero)
Bogotá's Chapinero neighborhood has become Colombia's fastest-growing startup corridor — combining corporate experience with entrepreneurial energy.
