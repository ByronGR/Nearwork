Why we exist

Nearwork was created because hiring should not feel like being left in the blue.

Too many companies are given one price, pushed into another, and then left to chase updates across emails, spreadsheets, and vendor calls. Too many candidates apply, wait, and never know what happened. Nearwork was built to change that.

Our team has worked inside this industry from both sides: the company trying to hire and the candidate trying to be seen. That experience shaped a platform and process where clients see the pipeline, candidates are evaluated with real evidence, and the relationship continues after the hire.

Transparent pricingNo surprise fees halfway through the process.
Visible pipelineYou see who is moving, why, and what evidence supports the shortlist.
Candidate respectPeople are saved for future matches, not thrown away after one decision.
Long-term supportRecruiting, EOR, managed teams, and support under one operating partner.
What makes us different

We are not here to take your money and hand you a resume.

Nearwork wants the hire to work, the partner to grow, and the candidate to succeed. That is why the system looks past the placement and into evidence, fit, support, and retention.

01

Proprietary matching system

Skills, role requirements, assessments, recruiter notes, salary expectations, and pipeline data are organized so hiring decisions are easier to defend.

02

Evidence before presentation

We do not wait until the opening closes. Candidates are reviewed while the role is active, so strong matches move faster and weak fits are documented.

03

Six-month replacement guarantee

If the hire does not work out under the guarantee terms, Nearwork supports the replacement process. That is how confident we are in the relationship.

We want to see your business grow.

Nearwork is here to be an extension of your team: recruiting, EOR, managed teams, support, and visibility in one place. Fewer vendors. Fewer surprises. Better outcomes.
