Our story
Our story

Built with a clear goal.

Nearwork was founded in 2024 with one purpose: help companies access high-quality talent in Colombia through a better, more structured hiring process. Not just faster — fundamentally better.

We've worked with local and international companies, supporting their growth by connecting them with professionals who are vetted, aligned, and ready to contribute from day one.

2024
2024 — Founded
Nearwork is born
Founded with the goal of changing how Colombian professionals connect with US SaaS companies. Built on the belief that hiring should be structured, transparent, and genuinely effective.
2025
2025 — Growth
Platform and process developed
The 7-stage vetting pipeline is established, the candidate bench grows, and first placements are made. The full marketing site, candidate portal, and client dashboard are built and launched.
2026
2026 — Expanding
Broader reach, same standard
Nearwork begins expanding its presence — reaching more companies and continuing to build out four-city coverage across Medellín, Bogotá, Barranquilla, and Bucaramanga.

Our philosophy

We actually care about the outcome.

For us, success isn't defined by placements made. It's defined by whether your hire works — long term. That distinction shapes everything about how we operate.

⏱️
We take the time to get it right
We don't rush placements to hit numbers. Every search runs through the same rigorous 7-stage process — regardless of how quickly a client needs the hire. Speed and quality aren't mutually exclusive when the process is built correctly.
🤝
We stay involved beyond the hire
Monthly check-ins for 6 months post-placement mean we stay accountable for the outcome. If something isn't working, we catch it early — before it becomes a problem that costs you time and money.
🔄
We continuously improve the process
Every placement teaches us something. We use feedback from clients and candidates to refine how we source, screen, and present — so each search is better than the last.
