About Nearwork

Focus on what matters:
growing your business.

Nearwork finds, vets and manages exceptional Latin American talent for US and Canadian teams — so hiring stops being a distraction and starts being an advantage.

Our story

From Colombia, for the world

2024 · The beginning

Founded in Colombia

Nearwork started at home — helping Colombian companies vet, source and hire the best local talent. We learned what great hiring really takes, one placement at a time.

2026 · Going international

Opening the door to the US & Canada

We shifted our focus to give US and Canadian companies the same advantage our local clients had: direct access to exceptional, English-fluent Latin American talent — in their time zone, at a fraction of the cost.

Today · 1,000+ placements

Trusted from SMB to enterprise

We have placed more than a thousand candidates, partnering with everyone from small businesses to enterprise teams to find the people who move their business forward.
