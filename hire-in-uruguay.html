A country worth hiring from

Calm, safe, and progressive, Uruguay punches astonishingly above its size. From Montevideo's rambla to the beaches of Punta del Este, its people are educated, reliable, and quietly excellent — Uruguay exports more software per capita than any country in Latin America, and its talent is prized for exactly that reliability.
