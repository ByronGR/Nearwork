Why 7 stages matter

Most hiring processes fail because they skip structure.

They move too fast, miss key signals, and leave you reviewing candidates who were never the right fit. Nearwork takes a different approach — every stage exists for a reason.

🔍
Each stage catches what the last one missed
No single evaluation is enough. Application review catches misaligned experience. English validation catches what a CV can't show. Role assessment catches what a conversation misses. Layers catch everything.
📉
You don't review the process — you get the result
You don't experience the 7 stages. We do. By the time you see a candidate, they've already passed every filter. You receive the output, not the work.
🎯
Structure produces consistency
A defined process means every candidate is evaluated the same way, against the same criteria, by the same standard — regardless of who's running the search.

Seven stages doesn't mean seven weeks. Our system is built to run stages in parallel where possible — no bottlenecks, no unnecessary delays.
