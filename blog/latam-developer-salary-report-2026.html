The nearshore salary advantage

Hiring in Latin America has become the default cost lever for US and Canadian teams — not because the talent is cheaper by compromise, but because the same skills carry a lower cost of living and no cross-continent time gap. A senior software engineer who might cost $150,000+ a year in the US is commonly available for roughly $55,000–$70,000 a year all-in through a nearshore hire — while working your exact hours.

Common questions

How much does it cost to hire a developer in Latin America?

Through Nearwork, full-time Latin American software developers typically range from about $1,750/month for junior roles to $4,750/month for senior roles, depending on stack and seniority — roughly 40–65% less than a comparable US hire. Exact rates vary by experience, industry, and exchange rate.

Is Latin American developer talent cheaper than US talent?

Yes. Comparable Latin American engineering talent typically costs 40–65% less all-in than a US hire, while working in the same US time zones. Nearwork charges a flat placement fee with no percentage of salary.

What is the average salary of a senior developer in LATAM?

Senior software developers in Latin America commonly fall in the $4,000–$6,500/month range depending on specialization (backend, data, cloud, mobile). These are representative monthly ranges for full-time hires; actual pay varies by country, experience, and FX.
