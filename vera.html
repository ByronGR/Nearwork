Vera · by Nearwork

Hear how they think.

Vera is Nearwork's audio-based situational-judgment assessment. It drops a candidate into real, on-the-job scenarios under time pressure — and scores how they actually think, not what they memorized.

The problem

A good interview isn't a good hire.

Polished résumés and rehearsed interviews tell you who prepared well — not who'll perform when the work gets messy. By the time you find out, you've already committed. Vera closes that gap.

How Vera works

Real scenarios. Real pressure. Real judgment.

No multiple choice, no script to memorize. Vera listens to how a candidate reasons in the moment — then turns it into a score you can compare.

1

Real scenarios

We drop the candidate into authentic, on-the-job situations for the exact role.

2

Under pressure

Time-boxed and audio-based — you hear how they reason live, with nowhere to hide.

3

Scored in plain bands

Their judgment is scored into clear, comparable bands, so you can rank a shortlist at a glance.

4

The full read

Every profile also includes a DISC behavioral profile and a verified English level.

What you get
