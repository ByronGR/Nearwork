A country built on English exposure

English proficiency in Colombia isn't accidental.

It's the result of decades of structured education, cultural exposure, and professional development — creating a workforce that speaks the language your team works in.

Primary school
Mandatory English curriculum from an early age — grammar, reading, and basic conversation.
Secondary school
Advanced English courses with increasing complexity — grammar, composition, and verbal expression.
University
English proficiency required for graduation in most programs. Many professors lecture in English.
English academies
A large private academy network runs from childhood through adulthood — continuously improving proficiency.
🎓
Mandatory in every school
English is part of Colombia's national education standard — not an elective. Every graduate has formal English training.
🏫
Academy network
Colombia has one of Latin America's largest networks of English academies, serving learners from age 4 through professional life.
🌍
Cultural immersion
Movies, music, digital media and global content consumed daily in English — building natural fluency beyond what a classroom provides.
💼
Professional environment
Tech and SaaS professionals in Colombia already work in bilingual teams — communicating with US clients every day.

How Nearwork does it

English isn't evaluated once — it's built into our process.

At Nearwork, English proficiency is not a checkbox. It's part of how we operate every day.

"We don't just test English — we work in it."
01
English-only environment
All internal communication at Nearwork happens in English — candidate instructions, feedback, evaluations, and support. This lets us assess real-world language in real conditions.
02
Real conversation evaluation
We don't just run a grammar test. We evaluate candidates through conversations, scenario responses, and written communication — the same things they'll do in your team.
03
Continuous assessment
English is evaluated at every stage of our process — from profile review through final presentation. If a candidate's communication doesn't meet C1, they don't move forward.
04
Written + verbal checks
We evaluate both written clarity and verbal fluency — because your team uses both every day. Emails, Slack messages, calls, and presentations all matter.
05
Real work scenarios
Candidates are assessed using scenarios they'll actually face — handling a difficult client call, summarizing a product update, explaining a process to a new teammate.
06
Demonstrated, not claimed
Any candidate can say they speak English. Nearwork candidates prove it — through every stage of our process, in the same conditions they'll work in with your team.
