The problem

Most hiring fails at the screening stage.

Resumes look good on paper. Interviews sound convincing. But without proper verification, you're making decisions based on assumptions — not facts.

📄
Inflated experience is common
Candidates extend tenures, inflate titles, and omit gaps. Without verification, you have no way to know if what you're reading is accurate.
🎭
Unverified skills don't reveal themselves until it's too late
A candidate can claim C1 English proficiency, Salesforce expertise, or CSM experience — and a basic interview won't catch the gap until they're already on your team.
📅
Inconsistent work history hides real problems
Frequent exits, employment gaps, and overlapping tenures are signals worth investigating — but most staffing companies don't look closely enough to find them.
⏳
Bad hires cost far more than the process that prevented them
Time lost onboarding, performance managing, and replacing a bad hire costs multiples of the salary — and the disruption to your team compounds the damage.
The real cost of a bad hire
Time spent onboarding4–6 weeks
Time lost performance managing2–4 months
Team disruption costHigh
Replacement search time3–6 weeks
Salary paid during notice/exit1–2 months
Estimated total cost of one bad hire 3–5× salary
The SHRM estimates the average cost of a bad hire at $14,900. For senior or specialized roles, it's significantly higher. A structured vetting process isn't overhead — it's risk management.
Our approach

Verify before we present.

Screening at Nearwork is not a quick review — it's a structured validation process. Before a candidate moves forward, we ensure their information is accurate, consistent, and aligned with your requirements.

📋
Structured, not ad hoc
Every candidate goes through the same structured process — not a different recruiter's gut feeling each time. The same checks, the same standards, the same bar on every search.
🔒
Verified, not assumed
We don't take claims at face value. We verify employment history, cross-reference work history timelines, confirm references, and test skills against real tasks — not checkbox answers.
🎯
Aligned, not generic
Verification is done against your specific role brief — not a generic talent standard. A candidate qualified for one company may not be qualified for yours. We screen for your requirements, not averages.
"If something doesn't check out — it doesn't move forward."
