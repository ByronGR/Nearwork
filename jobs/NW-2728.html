Senior Project Manager

Remote

Plan, execute, and deliver cross-functional projects on time, on scope, and on budget.

Responsibilities

  • Define project scope, timeline, resources, and success metrics
  • Manage project plans, risk logs, and stakeholder communication
  • Facilitate sprint/status meetings and remove blockers for the team
  • Coordinate across engineering, design, and business teams
  • Report progress and risks to leadership/clients
  • Ensure quality and adherence to defined processes (Agile/Scrum/Waterfall)

What we're looking for

  • 5+ years of project management experience, ideally in tech/software delivery
  • PMP, CSM, or equivalent certification preferred
  • Proficiency with project tools (Jira, Asana, MS Project, Monday.com)
  • Strong stakeholder management and English communication skills
  • Experience managing distributed/remote teams a plus
