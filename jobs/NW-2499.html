Customer Support Representative

Remote 1–3 years

We are sourcing Customer Support Representatives to join managed support teams for US-based companies across various industries. In this role, you will be the first point of contact for customers, resolving inquiries, troubleshooting issues, and delivering a consistently excellent experience. If you are empathetic, solutions-oriented, and thrive in a structured support environment, we want to hear from you.

Responsibilities

  • Handle inbound customer inquiries via email, chat, and/or phone
  • Resolve product or service issues efficiently and with empath
  • Escalate complex cases to the appropriate team following established protocols
  • Document interactions accurately in CRM or ticketing systems
  • Maintain a high level of customer satisfaction (CSAT) and first-contact resolution

What we're looking for

  • 1+ year of experience in customer support, call center, or BPO role
  • Strong English communication skills — written and spoken (B2 minimum)
  • Experience with helpdesk or ticketing tools (Zendesk, Freshdesk, Intercom, or similar)
  • Patient, empathetic, and solution-focused mindset
  • Ability to follow structured processes and document interactions
