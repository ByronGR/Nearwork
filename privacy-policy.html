Section 1

Introduction

Nearwork ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share your information when you use our platform and services ("Services").

By using Nearwork, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the platform.

Section 2

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information depending on how you use the platform:

a. Personal Information
  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Location
b. Professional Information (Candidates)
  • Resume / CV
  • Work experience
  • Skills and qualifications
  • Interview notes and evaluations
c. Business Information (Clients)
  • Company name
  • Contact details
  • Hiring requirements
d. Technical Information
  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device information
  • Usage data (pages visited, features used)
Section 3

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and improve our Services
  • Match candidates with job opportunities
  • Communicate with users about their applications and accounts
  • Manage accounts and process applications
  • Ensure platform security and prevent fraud
  • Comply with legal obligations
Section 4

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information in the following circumstances:

a. With Clients (Candidates)

Candidate profiles — including experience, assessments, and evaluation notes — may be shared with potential employer clients as part of the hiring process. This sharing occurs only when a candidate is actively being considered for a role.

b. With Service Providers

We use third-party tools to operate our platform. These providers process data only as necessary to deliver their services:

  • Firebase — data storage and authentication
  • Vercel — platform hosting
  • Resend — email communication
  • Stripe — payment processing
c. Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if required by applicable law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Nearwork, our users, or others.

Section 5

Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to:

  • Provide our Services
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Resolve disputes and enforce agreements

Candidate profiles may be retained beyond the conclusion of a specific role search in order to consider candidates for future opportunities. Users may request deletion of their data at any time — see Section 8.

Section 6

Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

However, no system is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, and we encourage users to use strong passwords and to keep their account credentials confidential.

Section 7

Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Object to or restrict certain types of processing

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address listed in Section 12. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

Section 8

Account & Data Deletion

You may request to delete your account or have your personal data removed from our systems at any time. To submit a deletion request:

📧
Send your request to
support@nearwork.co

Please include your full name and the email address associated with your account. We will process your request within 30 days, subject to any legal obligations that may require us to retain certain information.

Section 9

Cookies and Tracking

We may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to:

  • Maintain your session and keep you logged in
  • Improve user experience and platform performance
  • Analyze how the platform is used

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of the platform.

Section 10

Third-Party Links

Our platform may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of those external sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party sites you visit.

Section 11

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the platform after changes have been posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

For significant changes, we may notify users via email or a prominent notice on the platform.
