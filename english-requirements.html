Why English matters

English isn't a requirement — it's part of your day.

Most of Nearwork's clients work with international teams based in the US. That means English isn't something you use once a week in a formal meeting — it's how you operate every single day.

🤝
You collaborate directly with US teammates
No translation layer. No middle person. You're on Slack, in Zoom calls, writing Confluence docs, and communicating in real time with people in New York, Austin, and San Francisco.
📬
Written communication is constant
Emails, Slack messages, CRM notes, support tickets, client updates — everything you write is read by English speakers. Clarity isn't optional.
📊
Your performance is judged in English
When your manager rates your work, writes your review, or gives you feedback — it happens in English. Being able to receive, process, and respond to that clearly is essential.

How we evaluate it

Real conversation. Real judgment.

We don't rely on certificates or written tests. We evaluate English through direct interaction — the same way you'd actually use it in the role.

📞
Live spoken evaluation
A Nearwork recruiter conducts a real conversation with you — not a scripted test. We assess fluency, vocabulary range, comprehension speed, and communication confidence.

✕

✕
TOEFL / IELTS certificates — we evaluate directly, not through third-party scores.
✕
Formal academic language — we want professional communication, not essays.

Still improving? That's okay.

The goal is progress — not perfection.

English is a skill that develops over time. If you're not at C1 yet, here's what we suggest — and how to know when you're ready to apply.

01
Apply when you feel ready — not before
We're honest in our evaluations. If your English isn't at the level needed for the roles we're filling, we'll tell you clearly — and let you know what you'd need to improve to be considered in the future.
02
Focus on real communication, not just grammar
The fastest way to improve is to use English every day — watch English content without subtitles, have conversations, write in English. Grammar fixes itself when you're immersed.
03
Practice in the context of work
Practice business vocabulary, customer scenarios, and professional emails — not just general English. That's the vocabulary that will matter in your evaluation and in the role.
04
Create your profile and come back
Creating a Nearwork account is free. Even if you're not ready today, having your profile in our system means we can reach out when your level is right for a role we're filling.
