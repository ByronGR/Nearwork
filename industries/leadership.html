FAQ

Hiring Leadership talent, answered.

Full-time or fractional?

Both. We can place a full-time leader to run a function, or a fractional director a few days a week to level up an existing team.

What functions can they lead?

Engineering, support, operations, marketing and more — matched to a leader with real experience running that function for US and Canadian companies.

Do I need a legal entity in Latin America?

No. Nearwork acts as the Contractor of Record and handles local contracts, payroll, benefits and compliance. Your hire works for you day to day; we're their legal employer.

How fast can I hire?

Most searches produce a scored shortlist in about 14 days. You interview the finalists you like and pick — no résumé piles.

What if a hire isn't the right fit?

You're covered by a 3-month replacement guarantee. If it isn't working out, we re-source at no extra cost.
