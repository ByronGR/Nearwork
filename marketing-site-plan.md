## What We Have

The imported Claude page is a strong homepage concept with:

- Floating navigation with service, industry, pricing, candidate, and about menus.
- Hero section focused on reducing labor costs through on-demand remote staffing.
- Animated product preview showing pipeline, candidate profile, DISC, radar, and comparison views.
- Stats marquee for savings, speed, guarantee, English standard, and visibility.

## What To Build Next

1. Clean the homepage copy so it matches Nearwork's actual service model:
   Direct Recruiting, EOR, Managed Team, MSP / NSPP, payroll, equipment, and client portal visibility.

2. Replace placeholder claims before production:
   Trustpilot, G2, SOC 2, GDPR, 40+ companies, and specific customer testimonials should be verified or reworded.

3. Split the site into real pages:
   Home, Services, Industries, Pricing, Cost Calculator, Why Colombia, How It Works, About, FAQ, Candidate pages, and Book.

4. Connect navigation:
   Client login should go to `https://app.nearwork.co`.
   Candidate login should go to `https://talent.nearwork.co`.
   Open roles should go to `https://jobs.nearwork.co`.
   Booking should eventually go to `https://www.nearwork.co/book`.

5. Convert into the final framework:
   If this becomes a Vercel project, move this into a Next.js app with shared brand tokens, reusable sections, and SEO metadata per page.
