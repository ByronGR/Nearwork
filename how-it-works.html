The problem

Hiring today is broken.

Most companies don't struggle because of talent. They struggle because of process — or the lack of one.

📥
Endless resumes, no structure
CVs pile up in inboxes with no scoring, no context, and no clear next step. Your team wastes hours sorting through noise instead of building a business.
⏳
Delays between every step
Days pass between sourcing, screening, and feedback. No clear owner. No clear timeline. Candidates lose interest and your role stays open.
🌫️
No visibility into progress
You ask "where are we?" and get a vague email. You have no idea how many candidates were reviewed, what stage they're at, or when you'll see anyone.
🔀
Recruiters working in silos
Disconnected from your actual team and goals. Updates scattered across calls, Slack, and email. Nothing lives in one place.
That's where Nearwork changes everything. ↓

Hiring improves when there's structure, visibility, and accountability.

Nearwork brings all three together — in one process, managed by one team, visible in one place.

🏗️
Structure
A defined, consistent process that every candidate moves through — no exceptions. Every step has a clear owner, a clear output, and a clear next action. Process removes the chaos.
👁️
Visibility
When you can see exactly where every candidate is — with notes, scores, and stage history — you make faster, more confident decisions. No black boxes. No blind trust.
🎯
Accountability
Nearwork owns the process from kick-off to hire. We're accountable for the shortlist, the timeline, and the outcome — including a 6-month guarantee that backs every placement we make.
⚡
Faster hiring cycles
First shortlist under 21 days. Structured process removes the delays that slow down traditional hiring.
🎯
Better candidate quality

Less internal workload
